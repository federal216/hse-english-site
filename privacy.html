Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Data we collect

  • Account: if you sign in with Google or Apple, Firebase Authentication processes your account ID and the name/email supplied by that provider. Apple may let you hide your email address. Guest sign-in creates an anonymous ID.
  • Contact details: after signing in you must enter your email address and mobile phone number (with country code). They are stored with your profile and in Firebase Firestore linked to your account, used for support, account security, purchases and resolving payment disputes, and may be attached to bug reports you send. The phone number can be verified by SMS code. They are deleted when you delete your account, except for the "Payment dispute records" below.
  • Payment dispute records: the terms version and time you accepted, your purchase transaction record, contact details and a usage summary (first/last use date, number of active days, number of AI feature calls) are stored on our server. They are used only to defend against and correctly assess a payment dispute or refund request, and may be shared with Apple / Google if needed. They may be kept after account deletion for the legal claim period (up to 5 years). Contact support with deletion requests.
  • Chat and profile: if you use chat rooms, your display name and messages are stored in Firebase Firestore.
  • Usage and energy data: gamification/energy data linked to your account is stored in Firestore.
  • Bug reports: when you send one, its subject/message, the screen name, app version, device/platform, interface language and a short recent error summary are stored in Firebase and emailed to the app owner and support inboxes. If present, the email linked to your account may be attached. Contact support to request deletion of a report.
  • Purchases: payment is handled by the App Store / Google Play; we never see your card details. The app uses the store's purchase response to unlock premium access and restore purchases. If you opt in on the purchase screen, your Firebase-verified account email and, if available, Firebase-verified phone number are stored with the verified transaction and emailed to HSE English support to help with purchase issues. This is optional and does not affect purchase access. You can ask support to delete the contact details.
  • Ads: the app may show Google AdMob ads; advertising ID and device info may be processed for ads.
  • Photo analysis: only if you choose to, a photo is sent to an AI service for analysis. Do not send photos of confidential or private areas.
  • AI analysis: if you use the analysis feature, the text/photo you submit is sent to an AI service. To limit abuse and cost, usage counters are kept per account and per a value derived from your IP address (hashed, not reversible); these counters are deleted automatically after about 3 days.
  • App verification: Firebase App Check (Play Integrity on Android) processes device/app integrity signals to verify the app is genuine.
  • Ad consent: in the European Economic Area and the UK, Google's consent form is shown for ad personalization; you can change your choice at any time.
  • Stored on your device: learning progress, favourites, language and appearance settings, and notification times.

How we use data

To run the app, manage your account and store purchases, provide chat, review reports you submit, and show ads. We do not sell your data. Bug reports are retained while needed for support and troubleshooting; contact support to request deletion.

Third parties

Google/Firebase (accounts, database, ads and user-initiated AI analysis), App Store / Google Play (payments and restores), and Gmail (delivery of reports to the support inboxes). Their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights and deletion

You can request access, correction or deletion of your data at the address below. You can delete your account in the app (Profile/Settings) or request deletion by email; your account and related data are then deleted. Uninstalling the app removes local data on your device.

Children

The app is not directed at children; we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.
