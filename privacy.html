Privacy Policy

HSE English · Last updated: 7 November 2026

This policy explains what data the HSE English mobile app (Android and iOS) processes, why, and what your rights are. The app is developed by İbrahim Karakoç (Türkiye). Contact: hseenglishdestek@gmail.com

1. What data we process

  • Account data: If you sign in with Google (or Apple on iOS), Firebase Authentication processes your account ID and the name and email supplied by that provider; with Google your profile photo may also be received. Apple may let you hide your email address. With “Continue as guest” no name or email is requested; only a random anonymous ID (Firebase) is created.
  • Contact details (optional): After signing in you may enter your email address and mobile phone number (with country code); this step is not mandatory (“Skip for now”). They are stored with your profile and in Firebase Firestore linked to your account, used to reach you for support and bug reports, and may be attached to a bug report you send. The phone number is not verified by SMS. They are deleted when you delete your account.
  • Chat and community features: The country and city you choose, your display name, the messages you write and your room membership are stored on Firebase (Google Cloud). Other users in the same room can see your messages. Do not share personal details such as phone numbers or addresses in chat.
  • Usage and energy data: Gamification and energy data linked to your account is stored in Firestore.
  • Problem reports: If you use the “Report a problem” form, your subject and message, the screen the report was sent from, your user ID, your email (if on your account or contact details), your phone number (if you entered one), platform (Android/iOS), app version, interface language and a short recent error summary are stored in Firebase and emailed to the app owner and the support team.
  • OHS photo and AI analysis: When you use these features, the photo you take or select, or the text you write, is sent to third-party AI service providers (Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, Together AI, NVIDIA, xAI) so that it can be analysed. Data is sent only when you use these features. Camera and gallery permissions are requested only for the photo feature. Do not send photos of confidential or private areas. To limit abuse and cost, usage counters are kept per account and per a value derived from your IP address (hashed, irreversible); these counters are deleted automatically after about 3 days.
  • App verification: Firebase App Check (Play Integrity on Android) processes device and app integrity signals to verify that the app is genuine.
  • Learning data and settings: Your learning progress, favourites, language and appearance settings and notification times are mainly stored on your device.
  • Advertising: Google AdMob may be used for rewarded ads and may process data such as the advertising ID and device information under its own privacy policy. In the European Economic Area and the UK, Google's consent form is shown for ad personalization; you can change your choice at any time.
  • Purchases: Payments are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. We do not see or store your card details; the app only receives whether a purchase is valid and uses it to unlock premium access and restore purchases.
  • Notifications: The daily word notification is optional and is generated locally on your device.

2. Why we use the data

To run the app, let you sign in, manage your account and purchases, provide chat and community features, perform photo and AI analysis, fix bugs, answer support requests and prevent abuse.

3. Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use only the providers needed to deliver the service: Google Firebase (sign-in, database, app verification), Google AdMob (ads), Google Play / Apple App Store (purchases and restores), Gmail (delivery of problem reports to the support inbox) and the AI providers listed above for the analysis features. These providers process data under their own privacy policies.

4. Retention

Account, contact and community data is kept while your account exists or until you ask us to delete it. Problem reports are kept for as long as needed to resolve the issue. Usage counters are deleted automatically after about 3 days.

5. Account and data deletion

You can delete your account in the app (Profile/Settings); your account and related data (chat messages, problem reports, contact and profile data) are deleted. You can also email hseenglishdestek@gmail.com with the subject “Account deletion”. If you signed in with Google, include your account email; if you used guest mode, include the app version if possible. Requests are handled as soon as reasonably possible. Uninstalling the app also removes the data stored locally on your device.

6. Your rights

Under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and other laws that apply where you live, you may access, correct or delete your data, object to its processing and ask which parties receive it. Contact us by email to do so.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 16. If you notice this has happened, write to us and we will delete the data.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published on this page and the date above changes.
